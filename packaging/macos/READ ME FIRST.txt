Installing Beer
===============

1. Drag "Beer" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.
2. Open Beer from Launchpad or your Applications folder.

The first time you open it, macOS may say it "cannot verify" Beer, because
Beer is free software that isn't registered with Apple. To open it anyway:

  - Click "Done" (or "OK") in that message.
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  - Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the message about Beer.
  - Enter your password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

You only need to do this once.

When Beer starts it offers to install Wine (the part that runs Windows
programs). Say yes, and enter your password if asked. On Apple Silicon Macs
this also installs Rosetta 2.

To play a Pokemon fan game, click "Add Pokemon Game" and choose the game's
folder or the .zip/.rar/.7z file you downloaded.
